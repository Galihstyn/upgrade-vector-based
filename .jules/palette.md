## 2024-06-08 - Accessible Toolbars and Dynamic Status Text
**Learning:** For dynamic UI components that update frequently but not urgently (like zoom percentage text that changes when users click zoom buttons), applying `aria-live="polite"` ensures screen readers announce changes without interrupting current tasks. For icon-only buttons in toolbars (like Undo, Redo, Zoom), combining `aria-label` on the `<button>`, `aria-hidden="true"` on the inner SVG icon (to prevent redundant readouts), and `focus-visible:ring-2` styling ensures they are both audibly descriptive and visibly focusable for keyboard users.
**Action:** Always verify that dynamic status texts are marked with `aria-live` and all icon-only buttons contain `aria-label`, an explicitly hidden inner icon, and `focus-visible` styling during toolbar implementation.
